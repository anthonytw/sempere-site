Support

Contact

  • Questions and bug reports: open an issue at github.com/anthonytw/sempere/issues. Please never paste a real key or real notes; a description of what you did and what happened is enough.
  • Security problems: report them privately, as described in SECURITY.md.
  • Feature requests and translation suggestions: issues are welcome at github.com/anthonytw/sempere/issues. Sempere does not accept outside pull requests.

Sempere is volunteer-run, so replies can take a while. There is no account and no server, so nobody can reset your key or read your notes.

Reporting a crash

  • iPhone, iPad: Settings, Privacy & Security, Analytics & Improvements, Analytics Data. Find the files named Sempere-<date>.ips, share one, and attach it to a GitHub issue.
  • Mac: Console.app, Crash Reports, or ~/Library/Logs/DiagnosticReports (Sempere-*.ips).
  • Optional: turn on "Share with App Developers" in the same Settings screen, and Apple forwards crash logs to the developer.

Frequently asked questions

I lost my device. Can you recover my notes?

Your notes are encrypted to keys that only you hold, and the developer has no copy. Your other devices and sync targets still have them, and the recovery kit printed when you created the vault lets a new device take over. If every device and the kit are gone, nobody can decrypt the notes. Keep the kit somewhere safe, and keep a backup. See Replace a lost or broken device.

How do I back up my notes?

Choose Back Up To when you create a vault, or Settings, Backups, Choose Backup Folder. Sempere writes a <Name>.sempere-backup snapshot of the encrypted files to that folder (another drive or cloud provider), copies only what is new each time, and can verify it. The CLI does the same with sempere backup. A backup is for recovery: devices do not sync from it. Restore it with Restore from Backup.

How do I use my notes on another device?

Give the vault a sync target (a shared .sempere folder in iCloud Drive is the easy way, or a WebDAV server), open it on the new device, and choose Join This Device. Approve it on the device that manages devices by typing the six words it shows. See Add a second device.

Does Sempere sync?

Yes. Each device keeps a complete copy of the vault, works on it offline, and syncs it both ways with a sync target: a shared .sempere folder in iCloud Drive or elsewhere, or a WebDAV server. Other devices open the target and join by approval. Edits from several devices merge, with no conflict copies. The CLI does the same with sempere sync webdav and sempere sync folder.

What is sent to the developer, or to anyone?

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of ours. Handwriting recognition and transcription run on your device. The app connects only to a WebDAV server you add. The system may download a speech model from Apple, which holds none of your data. Read the privacy policy.

Which devices do I need?

iPadOS 27, iOS 27 or macOS 27 or later for the app; macOS 27 or later or Linux for the CLI. Write with Apple Pencil on the iPad, a finger on the iPhone, and the mouse or trackpad on the Mac. See the specs.

Can I import my Notability notes?

Yes. The importer reads the raw notes of a personal Notability backup (.note and .ntb files, their folders, or the backup's zip files), not PDF exports. It brings strokes, PDF backgrounds, images, typed text, recordings and recognized text, and skips notes already imported. It is in the app (File menu on the Mac, the note list's toolbar elsewhere) and in sempere import notability.

Can I read my notes without Sempere?

Yes. A note is a folder of revisions, and each revision is gzip-compressed JSON encrypted with age, so with your recovery key, age 1.3 or later, gunzip and jq you can read it. The recovery kit prints the steps. The sempere CLI exports PDF, SVG, PNG, Markdown, HTML and media files on macOS and Linux, and the web viewer opens a vault in a browser.

Why does handwriting search not find a word?

Recognition runs on the device (Apple Vision) and is on by default; check that Settings, Writing, Recognize Handwriting is on. "Recognize N Notes Now" in the note list's Handwriting menu reads every note still waiting, and so does the CLI on macOS (not on Linux). Search covers recognized text, titles, tags, notebooks, typed text, PDF text and, when you turn it on, transcripts. Transcription of recordings is off by default: turn it on in Settings, Audio & Transcription. Recognition can also misread a word.
