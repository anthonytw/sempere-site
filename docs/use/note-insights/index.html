Using Sempere

Note Insights

Summaries, keywords and related notes for each note, made on your own devices. What it makes, which devices make it, how to fix a device that is not ready, and how to turn it off.

Note Insights writes a short summary and keywords for each note, finds related notes, and summarizes whole notebooks and tags. Apple’s on-device model makes all of it, on your devices only; nothing is sent anywhere. It is off until you turn it on.

Turn it on

Open Settings ▸ Note Insights and turn on Note Insights. The vault must be unlocked and writable. The first pass is slow: plan on about an hour of charging for 500 notes. Your notes are not changed.

Sempere may also offer it once, with Turn On and Not Now.

What you get

  • A note’s insights. In a note’s More menu, or the note list’s context menu, choose Note Insights…. It shows the note’s summary, its keywords and its related notes. Regenerate makes them again on this device. A note with no text yet says “No summary yet”.
  • Search. Search also finds notes by their summaries and keywords. A result says “Matched in summary:” or “Matched in keywords:”.
  • Ask. Ask your notes uses the vectors to find notes by meaning, not only by your words.
  • Map of related notes. In All Notes’ menu, or Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Map of Related Notes. Notes sit in labeled groups that split into smaller groups as you zoom in. Lines join related notes, and a tap opens a note. A note this device placed itself is drawn dashed. A list form shows the same groups, for VoiceOver and small screens.
  • Topic summaries. A notebook or tag with at least 3 summarized notes gets Summary in its menu: a generated paragraph and the notes to check it against, marked when those notes changed. Each is made in the background at most once a day; Regenerate makes one now. Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Topic Summaries lists them all.

A summary is a machine’s reading of your note, not your words. Check it against the note.

Which devices make them

Every device with Apple Intelligence makes summaries and keywords. An iPhone or iPad does it while charging, or at once with Process Now (Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Status; it says why when there is nothing to make). A note is processed once its text has been unchanged for 10 minutes.

The insights are stored in the vault, encrypted like your notes, so every other device shows what these devices made, including devices that cannot run Apple Intelligence.

Any device that can do a job does it. There is no device to pick:

  • a device with Apple Intelligence makes the summaries and keywords that no device has made yet;
  • a device with the embedding model makes the vectors that no device has, and rebuilds the map of related notes when it is due (when more than a tenth of the notes changed, or weekly);
  • any device names voice notes.

Maps built at the same time are identical, and a summary or title made on two devices at once settles to the same one everywhere. A device whose embedding numbers drift from the map’s keeps its vectors to itself and never writes the shared set. Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Devices lists each device and what it can do.

Voice note titles. A quick voice note, from your iPhone or Apple Watch, gets no title written into it. Until you give it one, the list and the editor show “Voice note” (or “Watch voice note”) with its date and time, in your language and the time zone it was recorded in. Once the transcript is summarized, Note Insights’ title takes its place in the list: it is kept beside the note, follows the note’s text, and is never written into the note. A title you type is always the one shown. The Title format in Voice Note Titles shapes it; the default is {date} {time} {slug}, and {slug} is a few words from the model. Changing the format makes the titles again. Notes from before 1.3 keep the titles they have, and still get a generated one while their title is the default. Update every device of the vault to 1.3: a device still on 1.2 writes generated titles into notes and shows a blank title for new voice notes.

New voice notes. A new quick voice note gets its summary as soon as it is transcribed, also on battery. To wait for power instead, choose Only while plugged in under Settings ▸ Audio & Transcription ▸ Quick Voice Notes ▸ Insights for New Voice Notes (the other choice is Right away, even when not plugged in). The setting appears while Note Insights is on, and is kept per device.

Insight Language

Settings ▸ Note Insights ▸ Insight Language sets the language of generated summaries and voice-note titles. Same as Note (the default) writes each in the language of its note. Pick one language to have every summary and title of the vault in it. Because that replaces what exists, Sempere asks first and says how many summaries and titles will be made again. They are remade a few at a time, on every device, while charging. Keywords stay as the notes have them. It is a shared setting, so it follows your other devices (Settings).

“Apple Intelligence is not ready”

If a device cannot make summaries, Note Insights says why under its switch. The common message is “Apple Intelligence is not ready on this device. It may still be downloading, or its language does not match this device’s language and region.”

Tap How to Fix for the steps:

  1. Open Settings ▸ Apple Intelligence & Siri (System Settings on a Mac) and turn on Apple Intelligence.
  2. Set the Apple Intelligence and Siri language and region to the device’s language and region. They must match exactly, region included: “English (United States)” does not match a device set to the United Kingdom.
  3. Wait while the model downloads.

A language mismatch never fixes itself; the device stays “not ready” until you change it. Meanwhile the device still shows the insights other devices make.

Turn it off

Turn off Note Insights, then Turn Off and Delete Insights. This deletes every summary, keyword and related-note link of the vault on every device. Your notes are not changed. Turning it on again makes them anew.

Privacy

The model runs on the device. Summaries, keywords and the map are written into the vault encrypted with the vault’s keys, like a note, so a sync target sees only encrypted files. A generated summary that names things its notes do not support is discarded.

With the CLI

sempere settings set insights.enabled true
sempere insights update          # make this machine's summaries now (macOS 26, Apple Intelligence)
sempere insights show NOTE       # a note's summary, keywords and state
sempere insights related NOTE
sempere insights search QUERY
sempere insights status
sempere insights topic --list    # notebooks and tags with at least 3 summarized notes
sempere insights map --format svg
sempere settings set insights.language es   # Insight Language; "same" for the default
sempere insights purge           # turn it off and delete the insights

Reading insights works on Linux too. Making them (update, topic --generate, map --rebuild) needs macOS 26 with Apple Intelligence. See the CLI.
