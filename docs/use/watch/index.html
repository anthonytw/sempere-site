Using Sempere

Voice notes on Apple Watch

Record a voice note from your wrist. It goes to the vault's inbox through your iPhone. What to do with held notes, and if you lose the watch.

An Apple Watch can record a voice note and send it to your vault’s inbox. It works like quick voice notes on the iPhone, from the wrist. The watch reads and edits nothing: it has no copy of your vault and no key that can read a note.

Turn it on

  1. On the iPhone, turn on Quick Voice Notes (Quick voice notes) for the vault the notes should go to.
  2. Pair an Apple Watch with the iPhone. Sempere is installed on the watch with the iPhone app.
  3. Open Settings ▸ Audio & Transcription ▸ Use on Apple Watch and turn it on. The switch only appears when a paired watch has Sempere.
  4. If the watch asks, open Sempere on it once to finish the setup. Allow the microphone for Sempere on the watch.

The watch saves notes to the vault named under the switch. If you change the vault for quick voice notes, the watch follows.

Record

All of these open Sempere on the watch and start recording:

  • the complication and the Smart Stack widget;
  • the Sempere Voice Note control;
  • the Action button, on a watch that has one (choose Sempere in the watch’s Settings ▸ Action Button);
  • Siri: “Record a Sempere voice note”. The phrase must include “Sempere”.

Opening Sempere from the app grid shows a large record button instead. The watch shows Recording with the time and a Stop button. The control only starts a recording; stop it with the button in the app, with Siri or with a shortcut. A note can be up to 60 minutes long.

When you stop, the watch encrypts the note and shows Saved. A note that cannot be sent yet waits on the watch, still encrypted, and goes out when the iPhone is reachable.

Where it goes

The iPhone puts each note in the vault’s Inbox. The next time you unlock the vault, it becomes a note titled “Watch voice note” with the date and time, with its transcript if you have transcription on. Settings shows Last note from Watch. A note for a vault other than the one quick voice notes use waits on the iPhone until you open that vault.

Held notes

The iPhone only adds a watch note to your inbox while Use on Apple Watch is on and the note was encrypted with the key the iPhone holds now. Any other note is held: it stays on the iPhone, never in your vault, and never syncs. Settings ▸ Audio & Transcription lists held notes one by one, with why each is held and when it came.

For each held note, choose Add… (after a confirmation) or Delete. Delete All removes them all. Nothing is added in bulk.

A note that came while the switch was off, or after the key changed, shows a warning and the button is Add Anyway…. If you lost a watch, such a note may be one the lost watch recorded: add it only if you recorded it yourself. A note that cannot be added at all can only be deleted.

The iPhone holds at most 200 notes (500 MB). Past that, the watch keeps the rest until you add or delete some.

If you lose the watch

The watch holds the same voice note key as the iPhone. It cannot read any note, but whoever has it could add voice notes to your inbox until you do these, in this order:

  1. Turn off Use on Apple Watch. The iPhone stops taking files from the watch at once, and holds anything that still arrives.
  2. Unpair or erase the watch with Find My.
  3. Replace the vault key, which retires the key the watch holds. Any change that gives the vault a new key does this, for example Replace Recovery Kit… on the device that manages devices (Managing devices and keys).

Being far from the watch is no protection: it can reach the iPhone through Apple’s servers, not only over Bluetooth.

With the CLI

sempere inbox watch held list, held add and held delete manage held notes from a computer. held add --all skips the notes that need a check. See the CLI.
